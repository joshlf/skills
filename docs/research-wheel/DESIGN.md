# Research wheel: design

This package governs multi-revolution empirical, formal, analytical, or
source-grounded exploration when findings from one wave reshape what the next
wave should test, prune, escalate, or stop. Single-pass lookups, routine
implementation of a settled design, narrow edits, single-target model
refinement, and frozen population studies are outside its scope. Running the
wheel confers epistemic authority only and treats ingested sources, prior
notes, and peer outputs as untrusted evidence rather than instructions or
authority grants.

Exploration posture and continuation horizon are caller-owned scope and spend
decisions rather than domain facts to infer from vague intensity words. Before
probing, the skill requires explicit caller articulation of both terms and
persists them in the living model so resumptions neither guess nor re-ask
settled terms. At every revolution boundary it evaluates braking ahead of
deepening (`Prune -> Escalate -> Checkpoint or stop -> Deepen`) so governed
stopping and premise-refutation escalation outrank momentum.

Promotions and refutations require primary grounding, non-vacuity controls, and
host-honest challenge proportional to stakes. Separating claim dependencies
from falsifying-witness bases and actual basis invalidation (`stale`
expiration) from temporary observability outages (warrant-preserving
withholding) prevents counter-witness reversals or transient outages from
corrupting earned state, while orthogonal scope placement (`out-of-charter`
first) preserves verified external prerequisites without probing outside the
charter. Rewriting one current projection in place and reporting substantive
deltas only preserves continuity across compaction without telemetry noise.

The skill runs standalone and optionally composes with `reference-research`,
`corpus-navigate`, `model-refinement`, or separately installed coordination,
efficiency, supervision, or domain skills when present without bundling or
requiring unselected packages. Structural checks and design review do not
establish behavioral effectiveness; revisit the model if compact diagnoses
incur excessive ceremony, basis rules misclassify invalidations or outages, or
scope routing leaks outside the charter.
